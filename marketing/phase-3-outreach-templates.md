# Phase 3 — Outreach Templates

General rule across all of these: **personalize the bracketed parts before sending, every time.** A template that's obviously copy-pasted gets ignored or blocked. Spend 30 seconds looking at their profile/business before you send.

**Cadence:** send once → if no reply in 4-5 days, send ONE follow-up → if still nothing, stop and move on. Don't send more than 2 messages total to the same person.

**Tracking:** keep a simple spreadsheet — Name, Company, Segment, Date contacted, Follow-up date, Status (No reply / Replied / Call booked / Not interested). Takes 30 seconds per row and tells you what's actually working after a few weeks.

## A note on volume vs. quality

At "a few hours a week," aim for **5-10 well-personalized outreach messages per week**, not 50 generic ones. A genuinely personalized message to 10 people will outperform a copy-pasted blast to 100, and it's far less likely to get you flagged/blocked on LinkedIn.
